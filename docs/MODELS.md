# Face models: selection and evidence

Research date: 2026-11-08. The application uses local OpenCV inference on CPU. The files are ONNX models; this build runs them through OpenCV DNN, not ONNX Runtime. Both models must be included in the application package. The choice is a practical provisional balance, not a demonstrated winner on the requested laptop and camera. Scores from different papers, datasets, protocols and thresholds are not directly comparable.

| Candidate | Evidence and trade-offs | Release decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ArcFace / InsightFace | ArcFace is a strong baseline. Official InsightFace model zoo offers ONNX weights including lightweight variants. The code is MIT, but its distributed pretrained models are explicitly limited to non-commercial research unless licensed separately. | Do not bundle those weights for a potentially commercial release. [InsightFace licensing](https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface#license). |
| AdaFace | Quality-adaptive training addresses difficult low-quality inputs. The authors' CVLFace table reports, on WebFace4M and IR101, IJB-C TAR at FAR 0.01% of 97.46% for AdaFace and 97.30% for ArcFace; TinyFace Rank-1 is 72.13% and 71.03%, respectively. These are author results, not a webcam comparison. Some weights' commercial and dataset terms remain unclear. | Promising accuracy, but available checkpoint rights and practical Windows CPU deployment need verification. [AdaFace paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Kim_AdaFace_Quality_Adaptive_Margin_for_Face_Recognition_CVPR_2022_paper.html), [model results](https://github.com/mk-minchul/CVLface/blob/main/cvlface/pretrained_models/README.md), [license question](https://github.com/mk-minchul/AdaFace/issues/175). |
| MagFace | The embedding magnitude is designed to reflect image quality, which can help reject poor inputs. Its authors publish IJB evaluation code and a pretrained model. Apache-licensed code does not automatically settle every dataset and checkpoint right; the larger models need device-specific CPU measurement. | Not selected without clear rights and a comparative deployment benchmark. [Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/html/Meng_MagFace_A_Universal_Representation_for_Face_Recognition_and_Quality_Assessment_CVPR_2021_paper.html), [repository](https://github.com/IrvingMeng/MagFace). |
| MobileFaceNet | A compact architecture suited to CPU/edge inference; Qualcomm describes a variant under one million parameters. Model checkpoints and datasets have distinct terms. Reported LFW scores alone do not establish performance on low light, impostors or photo attacks. | Size is attractive, but no specific suitable checkpoint with adequately verified rights and comparable deployment results was established. [Qualcomm model card](https://huggingface.co/qualcomm/MobileFaceNet). |
| OpenCV SFace | Published as a compact local ONNX model. OpenCV Zoo provides SFace and YuNet examples and labels all files in the SFace directory Apache 2.0. This model has less directly comparable independently verified accuracy evidence than desired. OpenCV's published terms and an open question about its exact training data and upstream authorization should both be considered. | Selected provisionally for integration, CPU operation and published license. A commercial distributor should resolve the outstanding provenance question before relying on these weights. [SFace model](https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_recognition_sface), [open rights question](https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/issues/318). |
| LVFace (ICCV 2025) | Newer model family published in ONNX and PyTorch; authors report 96.67% IJB-C TAR at FAR 0.0001 for their T model. This is not measured on our application or hardware. Model weights are explicitly non-commercial research only. | Do not bundle for possible commercial use. [Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/You_LVFace_Progressive_Cluster_Optimization_for_Large_Vision_Models_in_Face_ICCV_2025_paper.html), [model and terms](https://github.com/bytedance/LVFace#license). |

NIST notes that image quality, lighting and pose can affect false negatives, while false positives can vary even with good image quality. An installation-specific evaluation must measure both; high LFW accuracy is not sufficient. See [NIST demographic effects](https://pages.nist.gov/frvt/html/frvt_demographics.html) and [NIST 1:1 verification](https://pages.nist.gov/frvt/html/frvt11.html).

## Calibration and presentation attacks

The cosine threshold of 0.363 is the [OpenCV example](https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/blob/main/models/face_recognition_sface/sface.py) starting point, not a calibrated threshold for this camera, user, multiple enrollment vectors or threat model. The app keeps vectors for 24 samples from normal light, dim light and changed head positions, computed both with and without contrast enhancement. Both sets are encrypted together. Matching and the blink challenge are separate, but the latter is a simple experimental heuristic and cannot establish that a face is live. Neither algorithm has been validated here against printed photos, phone screens, replay video, impostors or camera failure. Moving a photograph is not proof of life.

Real locking requires a registered face and one explicit confirmation for each session; confirming also starts protection if it is stopped. Photo protection is enabled by default but is optional and remains experimental. The user reported testing an unregistered person and photos; the conditions and error rates were not independently recorded. Continue supervised tests with the owner in good and poor light, with glasses and varied positions; at least one unregistered person; printed and phone photos; unusably dark images; and the intended webcam at its practical resolution. Record false acceptance, false rejection, timing and RAM under a defined protocol with adequate samples. Do not infer a statistically meaningful error rate from a few checks. Keep test mode until the results and limitations are understood. Desktop automation may not work while Windows is locked.
